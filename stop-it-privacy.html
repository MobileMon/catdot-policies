Stop it Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Stop it Android TV game handles information.

Information in the app

Stop it does not require an account and does not collect or send personal information to us. The app does not use ads, analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting. It saves scores, the number of free runs used, and purchase status on your device.

Retention and deletion

Your device keeps these records until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Clearing app data resets local scores and trial records. Google Play manages purchase records separately.

Google Play

Google Play processes purchases and may process information when you download, purchase, or manage the app under its own privacy policy. The app checks Google Play for purchases to unlock or restore the full version. We do not receive your payment details.

Contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to respond and handle your request, and retain messages as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion.

For privacy questions, contact rcsd.feedback@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app changes.
