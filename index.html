Cat Dot Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy explains how the Cat Dot Android TV app handles information.

Information in the app

Cat Dot does not ask you to create an account, and the app does not collect or send personal information to us. It does not use ads, analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting.

The app saves your light or dark appearance choice and purchase status on your device. If the free trial is enabled, it also saves the amount of trial time used on your device. These records are not sent to us by the app.

Retention and deletion

Your device keeps the app's saved settings and trial record until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Clearing app data also resets your saved settings and trial record.

Google Play

Google Play processes purchases and may process information when you download, purchase, or manage the app under Google's own privacy policy. The app checks Google Play for purchase information to unlock and restore the full version. We do not receive your payment details.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include in your message. We use that information to respond and handle your request. We keep messages as needed for support and applicable legal obligations; you may contact us to request deletion.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will show when the current version took effect.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Cat Dot support at rcsd.feedback@gmail.com.
